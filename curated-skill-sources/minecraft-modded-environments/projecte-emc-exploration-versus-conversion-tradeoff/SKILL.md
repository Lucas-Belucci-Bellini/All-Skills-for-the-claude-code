# ProjectE Exploration Versus Conversion Tradeoff

## Purpose

source locations, route prerequisites, candidate EMC, renewable alternatives, and pack design goals

## Required inputs

Identify what discovery or exploration action is intended to provide the resource and whether EMC bypasses it completely.

## Workflow

1. Confirm Minecraft, loader, ProjectE, and modpack versions from the actual target instance; capture exact IDs and config sources. If version evidence is unavailable, stop before proposing changes.
2. Compare direct acquisition and conversion as alternate paths; record which incentives should remain meaningful.
3. A tradeoff table with preserved exploration goals, acceptable conversion role, and exceptions.
4. Validate the finding against a disposable fixture, repeatable calculation, or independent data lookup; compare current and proposed behavior and mark unknowns explicitly.
5. Produce do not assume that all exploration rewards must be protected; tie decisions to the stated pack profile. with evidence references, confirmed-versus-proposed status, affected IDs, assumptions, confidence, and the next safe action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The target version and evidence source are recorded; unknown version details are not guessed.
- [ ] The topic-specific behavior or data relationship has a reproducible check.
- [ ] Inputs, quantities, units, gates, or other assumptions relevant to the decision are explicit.
- [ ] Proposed values are distinguished from confirmed values and include rationale or a review-needed status.
- [ ] No production config/world is changed without backup, scoped diff, and rollback path.

## Required output

Do not assume that all exploration rewards must be protected; tie decisions to the stated pack profile.

## Boundaries and failure handling

An EMC assignment may eliminate the incentive to visit a dimension or structure. If recipe, registry, format, or runtime evidence is incomplete, mark the result unknown and name the smallest additional evidence needed. Do not claim universal coverage or successful balancing without measurable validation.

## Trigger examples

- **Positive:** The resource is a common crafting input with no exploration role.
- **Negative:** undefined
